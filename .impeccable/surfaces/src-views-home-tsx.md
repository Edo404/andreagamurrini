# Home (one-page) of dr.gam

Mode: persuade. Audience: fans arriving from social, YouTube, Spotify, mostly on phones. Action: listen to "Live in Rome" on Spotify. Proof: real releases, videos, photos, press. Constraint: the Press section is frozen (src/app/press.css) and must stay pixel-identical. Seed key: f36c5a46 (assigned index 4).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a sun-faded wall on Fuerteventura, painted by hand the way a beach bar paints its board: dr.gam's music advertised in sign paint. It refuses the dark grid-of-cards artist template.

OWN-WORLD: Atlantic blue painted ground in three depths, whitewash panels, sun yellow and bougainvillea pink sign paint, painted wave stripes, sun discs that double as vinyl, ticket stickers. Shrikhand sign lettering for headings only, Figtree for everything read.

STORY: hear him (Live in Rome), see him (videos), the records, his words, the photos, the press that wrote about him, write to him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the painted dr.gam sign over a blue duotone portrait set inside a yellow sun disc, a Live in Rome ticket with a working Spotify button visible without scrolling.

SIGNATURE: a painted wave line that draws itself along each section edge as you scroll.

RISK: sign lettering turns kitsch at small sizes, so it never goes below heading size; the frozen dark Press band must be reached through deliberate transitions.
