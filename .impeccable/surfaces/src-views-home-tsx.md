# Home (one-page) of dr.gam

Mode: persuade. Audience: fans and listeners arriving from social, YouTube, Spotify, mostly on phones. Action: listen to "Live in Rome" on Spotify. Proof: real releases, videos, live photos, press. Identity: Rock/Pop/Funk with a strong Rock/Pop live soul ("Live e manifesto"). The round red/white logo stays as it is. Press is no longer frozen: it joins the world. Seed key: 71295601 (assigned index 6).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the opened gatefold sleeve of the live double LP "Live in Rome": it opens on the stage, and every section is a panel, inner sleeve or insert of the record. It refuses the category default (full-bleed dark stage photo under condensed caps and a red button).

OWN-WORLD: Uncoated cool-grey sleeve board as ground; two spot inks only, Rome red and stage blue, overprinting to near-black; live photos printed as one-ink halftones (blue, or black on red); the round logo as the vinyl's centre label; liner-notes credit columns; a fold line. Wide expanded grotesk for titles (Archivo, wide and black), the same family at normal width for everything read.

STORY: open the sleeve and press play (Live in Rome), read the liner notes (facts), pull out the records, watch the stage, read his words and the press clippings, see the contact sheet of photos, turn the sleeve over to write to him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: two panels across a fold: left, a stage photo in blue halftone bleeding over the fold; right, the Rome-red panel with "dr.gam" in wide black letters, "Live in Rome" with its cover, the logo-label vinyl half out of the sleeve, and a working Listen on Spotify button. Phone: photo panel on top, red panel below, Spotify above the fold.

FORM: Live double-LP gatefold sleeve, position 6 on the ordered list, seed key 71295601. Raises: riso (only two inks plus overprint black), darkroom (photos develop out of the halftone as they enter), pasteup (press clippings slap down, slightly crooked).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
